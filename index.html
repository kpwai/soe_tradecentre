Welcome to SMU Centre for Research on International Trade

The SMU Centre for Research on International Trade promotes research that analyses the complex network of firm-to-firm connections in Singapore and other Asian economies. By analysing how external shocks propagate through these networks, the Centre aims to shed light on Singapore’s integration into the global economy and the ways global shocks influence firm productivity, labor demand, and innovation activities within the country. These insights are crucial for developing strategies to enhance the resilience and competitiveness of Singapore’s economy in an increasingly fast-changing and uncertain global environment.
